A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
step in.

What is a chapter?+

A chapter is the part of life you’re living within a Space, like starting over in a new city or building something new in Career. It can change as you do.

Is Grouv free?+

Yes. You can join and use Grouv for free. Season Pass is an optional paid membership if you want more.

Can I keep my Log private?+

Yes. You choose what stays just yours and what you share. Your Log should never become public simply because you joined a Space.

Is this another followers app?+

Grouv is designed around the chapter you’re living and the people who can relate to it — not building an audience or curating a perfect image.
